About the Project

The Polar Caves Project is an ongoing effort to survey, map, and characterize cave systems in some of Earth's most remote polar environments. Working at the intersection of glaciology, speleology, and geoscience, the project contributes new data on subglacial hydrology, ice dynamics, and carbonate geology in the high Arctic. Fieldwork is conducted under scientific permits in collaboration with international research partners.
